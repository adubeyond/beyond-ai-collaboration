# Code of Conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in BEYOND a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community, not only individual preferences.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, Issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

Maintainers must apply these standards fairly. A maintainer who is directly involved in a report must recuse themselves from handling it whenever another qualified reviewer is available.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents BEYOND in public spaces. Project spaces include the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, reviews, and community communication channels.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to [adubeyond@qq.com](mailto:adubeyond@qq.com) with the subject `[BEYOND Code of Conduct]`. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public Issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. The privacy and safety of the reporter and affected people will be respected. Receiving a report does not guarantee a particular outcome or response time.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may use these community impact guidelines when deciding consequences:

1. **Correction:** A private written warning and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with conditions on continued participation and a defined period of no contact with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban:** A temporary ban from interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban:** Permanent removal from project participation for sustained violations, repeated disregard of community safety, harassment, or aggression toward individuals.
